Unity Asset Store price tracking, without another account

Use Asset Sale Watch as a Unity Asset Store price tracker, sale alert, publisher watcher, and deal-discovery dashboard. It is built for Unity developers who want to catch flash deals, launch discounts, publisher sales, and sale-ending deadlines without manually checking the store every day.

What can it track?

You can watch individual assets, publishers, new-release discounts, sale deadlines, and price drops. During big Unity Asset Store sales, it helps catch rotating flash deals before they disappear.

Does it collect my watch list?

No. Your watched assets, notes, tags, and settings stay in your browser's local storage. There is no account, no telemetry, and no server-side watch list.

How it's funded

Asset Sale Watch is free. Some links it generates — and the deal links on this site — are Unity Asset Store affiliate links: if you buy through them, we earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. The extension never modifies the Asset Store pages you browse, never injects anything, and clearly discloses its affiliate links. Community share features deliberately produce clean, non-affiliate links.
