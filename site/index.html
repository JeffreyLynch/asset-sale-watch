Never miss a Unity Asset Store sale again.

Asset Sale Watch is a free, local-first browser extension that watches the assets and publishers you care about and alerts you the moment a sale starts — or before one ends.

Add to Chrome — Free Browse today's deals

Sale-end countdowns

See exactly when every watched sale ends, with reminders at 48h, 24h, 6h, and 1h — flash deals rotate daily during big sales, and they're designed to be missed.

Publisher watching

Follow your favorite publishers and get notified when they discount anything — including Publisher of the Week sales. No other tool does this.

Price-drop alerts

Watch any asset, even ones not on sale. The moment it enters a sale or drops in price, you get a browser notification.

New-release discovery

Browse launch discounts and recently published assets with filters for category, discount depth, and price — without opening a dozen store tabs.

Local-first privacy

No account, no telemetry, no server. Your watchlist, notes, and tags live in your browser. Backups are plain JSON files you control.

Free & open source

The full source is public and auditable. Read the code on GitHub.

How it's funded

Asset Sale Watch is free. Some links it generates — and the deal links on this site — are Unity Asset Store affiliate links: if you buy through them, we earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. The extension never modifies the Asset Store pages you browse, never injects anything, and clearly discloses its affiliate links. Community share features deliberately produce clean, non-affiliate links.
